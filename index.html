ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Built around the idea that knowledge travels better together.

COFsConnect is a student-focused academic community platform designed to make it easier to access notes, share knowledge, stay updated, discover college events, share photographs, ask seniors questions and find useful academic resources.

The visual language combines contemporary digital design with restrained classical architectural details: arches, columns, stone tones and balanced proportions.
