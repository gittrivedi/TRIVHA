THE COMMUNITY

About COFsConnect

COFsConnect is a student-focused academic community platform designed to make the everyday flow of college information easier to discover, share and revisit.

It brings together college updates, study resources, practical material, presentations, assignments, campus photographs, events and peer-to-peer questions in one calm, searchable space.
